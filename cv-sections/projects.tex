\cvproject
{Nabz}
{Lab Reports Explained}
{Aug -- Oct 2026}
{Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL 17, pgvector, RapidOCR, Groq, React, TypeScript, Three.js, Docker}
{github.com/Bishnu2430/Nabz}
{Complete}
\begin{itemize}
\item Reads a photo or PDF of a blood-test report, has the user confirm every value, then explains the results in plain English, Hindi or Odia, maps them onto an interactive 3D body and tracks them across years. Built to help people prepare for their doctor; it does not diagnose.
\item Extraction: the PDF text layer first, else RapidOCR (ONNX, CPU) after projection-profile deskew, then a layout-agnostic row parser, LOINC matching over a 108-test catalogue, unit normalisation and a trained row-confidence model that sends weak rows to review first. 100\% field accuracy on 2,228 rows from digital PDFs; 94.6\% row recall at 100\% precision on simulated phone photos.
\item Analysis: lab-range classification, fixed critical-value alerts, reference change values, Theil--Sen trends with an exact Mann--Kendall test, and NHANES population percentiles. On 50 synthetic histories it confirms 88\% of planted drifts at five visits with 1.0\% false alarms.
\item Explanations: gpt-oss-120b writes to a strict JSON schema from retrieved NLM MedlinePlus passages in pgvector; a deterministic validator (script, numbers, citations, banned intents in three languages), then an LLM judge, then a template fallback. No generated text appears near a critical value; 77 red-team cases run in CI.
\item Platform: API and worker queue, 22-table schema with Alembic, accounts with two-step sign-in for staff, doctor sharing by QR link, data export and deletion, an OWASP ASVS 5.0 Level 1 self-assessment, 13 architecture decision records, and CI for backend, frontend and diagrams.
\end{itemize}

\cvproject
{Worldline}
{Relativistic Universe Sandbox}
{Oct 2026 -- present}
{Rust, wgpu, egui; data from NASA JPL and NAIF}
{github.com/Bishnu2430/Worldline}
{In progress (pre-alpha)}
\begin{itemize}
\item Desktop engine that runs the real solar system in 3D: 459 moons, 62 dwarf planets, asteroids and comets, 28,331 belt objects, the solar wind, heliosphere and magnetospheres. Users drop black holes, neutron stars and planets into it and watch general relativity play out.
\item Gravity: Einstein--Infeld--Hoffmann relativistic n-body dynamics with an adaptive IAS15 integrator, planets' gravity fields, post-Newtonian binaries with radiation reaction, and motion in exact Kerr spacetime. Earth stays within 0.41 km of NASA JPL after a simulated year (61 km without relativity).
\item Every physics model ships with a validation test against theory or observation, 65+ passing: Mercury's relativistic precession 42.9807$''$ vs.\ 42.9805$''$ per century; the Hulse--Taylor pulsar's orbital decay within 1.1$\sigma$ of the measured rate; a GW150914 replay leaving 63.0 M$_\odot$ with spin 0.68 (LIGO: 62 $\pm$ 4, 0.67); Halley's Comet returning 17 minutes from JPL's 2061 time.
\item Compact objects from first principles: the Chandrasekhar limit (1.456 M$_\odot$), a 2.048 M$_\odot$ maximum neutron-star mass from the SLy equation of state, and Hawking evaporation.
\item GPU work in wgpu: ray-traced shadows of spinning black holes with Doppler-shifted accretion disks, and SPH fluid planets that tides tear apart, 8,144 particles at 32--55 frames a second on integrated graphics.
\end{itemize}

\cvproject
{Q-CONSENSUS}
{Multi-Agent LLM Reasoning with Verifiable Decision Trails}
{2026}
{Solo. Python, FastAPI, llama.cpp, Qiskit Aer, Solidity, Geth, React, Docker}
{github.com/Bishnu2430/Q-CONSENSUS}
{}
\begin{itemize}
\item Five agents debate a question on a local LLM served by llama.cpp; a QAOA solver over quantum-kernel similarity between their answers picks the consensus, benchmarked against a classical baseline.
\item Every event is SHA-256 hash-chained and anchored by a Solidity contract on a private Ethereum (Geth) chain, so any run can be replayed and verified after the fact.
\item FastAPI service with a React front end; pytest suites cover the debate flow, the quantum optimiser, contract anchoring, reconciliation and API security.
\end{itemize}

{github.com/Bishnu2430/Flood\_Detection\_System}
{}
\begin{itemize}
